## Why Build This When Alternatives Exist?

None of the existing apps combine all three pillars:

1. **Dictation apps** (Scribe, open-wispr, VoiceInk) — speak to text, but your words vanish after insertion. No memory, no searchability, no learning.

2. **Note apps** (Apple Notes, Obsidian) — great for organizing, but no voice-first input or AI cleanup.

3. **AI assistants** (ChatGPT, Claude) — smart but cloud-based, no local memory, no dictation integration.

**Seshat's thesis:** Your voice is the most natural input. Every word you dictate should be captured, searchable, and useful — not just pasted and forgotten.
