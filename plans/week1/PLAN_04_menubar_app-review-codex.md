VERDICT: NEEDS_REVISION

## Summary Assessment
Contract awareness is strong, but the plan still has three blocking problems: a non-compilable `stateStream()` seam, a record path that bypasses app-owned permission flow, and a manual-launch story that contradicts the rejected Option B wiring.

## Critical Issues (must fix)
- `MenuBarSceneModel`'s observation seam does not match the authoritative `SessionCoordinator` actor API. Plan 00 defines `SessionCoordinator.stateStream()` as an actor method (`public func stateStream() -> AsyncStream<SessionState>`) and shows external use as `let stream = await coordinator.stateStream()` ([PLAN_00_common-final.md](./PLAN_00_common-final.md) C.3, lines 312-323; Step 9.2, lines 765-779). Plan 04 instead injects `stateStream: @escaping @Sendable () -> AsyncStream<SessionState>` and then consumes it synchronously in `for await state in stateStream()` ([PLAN_04_menubar_app.md](./PLAN_04_menubar_app.md) lines 226-240, 527-535). That closure cannot call the actor-isolated method without `await`, so the prescribed API either fails to compile or pressures the implementer into inventing a wrapper, which Plan 00 D.7 forbids ([PLAN_00_common-final.md](./PLAN_00_common-final.md) lines 567-576). The seam must become async or the model must consume `SessionCoordinator` directly.
- The record action currently routes around the app-owned permission flow. Plan 00 says mic prompting belongs to app/UI flow, not `PSAudio` ([PLAN_00_common-final.md](./PLAN_00_common-final.md) lines 560-562, 866). But Plan 04 keeps `RecordButtonViewModel` "free of permission knowledge" ([PLAN_04_menubar_app.md](./PLAN_04_menubar_app.md) lines 461-463), always renders the record button even when permission is not granted ([PLAN_04_menubar_app.md](./PLAN_04_menubar_app.md) lines 304-317), and Step 5 explicitly requires the tap to reach `SessionCoordinator.toggle()` "and nothing else" ([PLAN_04_menubar_app.md](./PLAN_04_menubar_app.md) lines 475-495, 844). That leaves the `.notYetRequested` / `.denied` path ungated and risks pushing prompt/failure ownership back into capture/session code. The plan needs an explicit rule: either disable record until `.granted`, or have `handleRecordButtonTap()` request permission first and only toggle after success.
- The development launch story is internally inconsistent. Plan 04 correctly selects Option A and rejects temporary fake-`@main` wiring ([PLAN_04_menubar_app.md](./PLAN_04_menubar_app.md) lines 45-52), while also deferring the thin production `@main` bootstrap to Plan 99 ([PLAN_04_menubar_app.md](./PLAN_04_menubar_app.md) lines 90-96, 854-857; [PLAN_00_common-final.md](./PLAN_00_common-final.md) lines 459-465, 804-832). But Step 12 still requires a real local runbook with `swift run PSAppKit` ([PLAN_04_menubar_app.md](./PLAN_04_menubar_app.md) lines 766-790, 865-868). As written, the plan promises a runnable executable entrypoint while explicitly refusing to define one. It needs a concrete, contract-compliant dev launch mechanism, or the manual-launch requirement must move to Plan 99.
